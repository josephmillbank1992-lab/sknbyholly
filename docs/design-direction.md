# Skn By Holly design direction

Reviewed against the existing Daniella, Skin Reset Clinic and Kelly Buxton repositories, including Daniella's `docs/design-standards.md`. That document identifies Mane London and Tailored Tools as foundations.

- Never use the rounded or arched Sophellie window composition for this project.
- Use Holly's requested Prata typography and warm paper, bone, stone and charcoal palette.
- Distinct composition: an oversized typographic opening, asymmetrical rectangular photography and two useful treatment routes.
- Keep images at their natural proportions, including on wide screens. No arbitrary portrait crops, floating rotated cards or ornamental italic headings.
- Prioritise treatment discovery, clear model prices and practical visit information.
- Carry a chosen treatment into an enquiry, following the useful catalogue-to-enquiry pattern in the reference work. Visitors review and send their message in WhatsApp.
- Keep online booking status honest until a verified booking installation is connected.
- Preserve approved imagery and content. Exclude the client-on-bed and treatment-in-progress photos.
- Keep text readable, keyboard controls usable and reduced-motion preferences respected.
